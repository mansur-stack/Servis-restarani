A kitchen that follows the market

Savoria started as a family table with one wood-fired oven. We still buy from local growers, bake bread daily and change a third of the menu every season.

  • Local first. Vegetables and herbs come from nearby farms.
  • Cooked fresh. Nothing is reheated; sauces are made each morning.
  • Made to share. Generous plates, relaxed pace.
